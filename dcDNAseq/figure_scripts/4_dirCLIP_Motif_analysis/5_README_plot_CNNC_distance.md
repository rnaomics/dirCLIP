# CNNC Distance Distribution Plotting

## Purpose

This script visualizes the spatial distribution of CNNC motifs relative to significant mutation sites.

The analysis compares the observed CNNC-distance distributions against two control strategies:

1. Sequence-shuffled controls
2. GC-matched random background controls

The script generates both:

- Frequency distributions
- Cumulative frequency distributions

of the nearest CNNC motifs around mutation sites.

## Workflow

```text
Mutation-site FASTA sequences
            ↓
CNNC motif identification
            ↓
Nearest CNNC distances
            ↓
Observed CNNC distribution
            ↓
Compare against:
    • Shuffled sequences
    • GC-matched random controls
            ↓
Generate frequency plots
            ↓
Generate cumulative frequency plots
```

---

## Analysis

For every mutation-site-centered sequence:

1. The nearest CNNC motif is identified.
2. The distance from the sequence center is calculated.
3. Distances are aggregated across all sequences.
4. Frequencies are normalized.
5. Control distributions are generated using:
   - shuffled sequences
   - random background sequences
6. Mean and standard deviation across control datasets are calculated.
7. The observed and control distributions are visualized.

## Figure Interpretation

```text
Actual_seq
```

Observed mutation-site sequences.

```text
random_mean
```

Average control distribution.

### Grey Ribbon

```text
random_mean ± random_sd
```

Variability across control datasets.

---

## Notes

- Distances are calculated relative to the center of the sequence window.
- Infinite distances correspond to sequences lacking any CNNC motif and are excluded from plotting.
- Random-background controls are GC matched.
- Shuffled controls preserve nucleotide composition.
